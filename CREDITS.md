## Privacy
No accounts, analytics, ads or network requests. Progress lives only in your browser's local storage (`bait-al-dumiah.v1`) and is never sent anywhere.

The size of memory (2026): original camera direction, bilingual captions, music-box synthesis and material foley produced locally for this project. The film reuses the game's own procedural house and resident art. No sampled music, voice track or third-party imagery. Three.js remains covered by its existing local MIT license.
