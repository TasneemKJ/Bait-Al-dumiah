# Competitive study: cozy home, craft and care games

Purpose: learn design principles from games close to Bait Al-dumiah, a cozy 3D Levantine dollhouse game about care, crafts and a shy visitor. We learn principles only. We never copy assets, names, text or distinctive content.

Source note: these notes come from general knowledge of the games, their store pages and widely reported player reception. They were not freshly checked against live sources in this session, so treat details as approximate.

## Lessons for Bait Al-dumiah, ranked by impact

1. **Tell players what is waiting today.** Pocket Camp and Cozy Grove open each session with a clear daily reason to act. We should say in one line what waits: basket, journal rewards, wishes. *Shipped this round: welcome-back line.*
2. **Teach by doing, not text.** Unpacking, A Little to the Left and Toca need no reading. Keep the clue folded on phones and let the action button and an unread dot carry the teaching. *Shipped: compact clue chip.*
3. **Every touch answers.** Toca and Townscaper make each object react. Audit untouched props; give each one a tiny wobble, sound or line. (Later)
4. **A distinct sound per object class.** Unpacking does this for placing items. Our music-box synth can give porcelain, cloth, wood and brass their own short tones. (Later; audio)
5. **Before/after reveals.** House Flipper and Cozy Grove's returning colour make progress visible. Our room restorations should get a soft reveal moment with the camera holding on the room. (Later)
6. **Gentle mischief instead of punishment.** A Little to the Left's cat creates small, charming disorder. The visitor could shift one keepsake overnight for the player to notice and fix. This fits cute-and-creepy without loss. (Later; dread and comedy)
7. **Daily cap with a story drip.** Cozy Grove's cap creates anticipation. We already cap rewards per day; the cap should also be visible as "tomorrow" content in the journal. (Later)
8. **Portrait-first, thumb-reach UI.** Pocket Camp puts primary actions low. We keep room tabs and tools at the bottom, and the HUD frames the whole house. *Shipped: whole-house framing and HUD gutters.*
9. **Avoid timers and paywalls.** These draw the most complaints in Pocket Camp and Sims FreePlay. Keep "no offline decay, no purchase" as a design rule. (Already a rule)
10. **Character-led goodbyes and arcs.** Spiritfarer's arcs give long-term meaning. The visitor's whispers can grow into a short arc with a final memory. (Later; expansion)
