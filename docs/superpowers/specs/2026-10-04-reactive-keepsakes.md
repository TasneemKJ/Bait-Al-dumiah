Keep rewards bounded and optional: no currency from repetitive object clicking, no lost rewards for missing a day, no external assets or analytics. More elaborate chapters/challenges should follow observation of these hands-on actions rather than burying interaction inside another menu.

Do not implement this queued iteration until the current software-WebGL flow passes, screenshots are inspected, and iteration 2 is committed as verified. Refine exact simulation contracts and write the implementation plan at that point. This is the twenty-idea brainstorm, not a claim that these ideas are shipped.
