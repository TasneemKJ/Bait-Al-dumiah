# Pacing curve, 2026-10-06

Numbers first. Produced by `node scripts/pacing-curve.mjs 7` (30 minutes, median over seeds 1-7; about 1 s to run; `tests/pacing-contract.test.mjs` keeps the script honest). Simulation only: it plays the pure rules in `src/simulation.js`. The tea, sewing and chime minigames are **excluded** (they need real touch input and their rewards are daily-capped), so every figure is a floor for earnings, not a total. Seeds change only the newcomer's hesitation; the expert is deterministic, so its seven runs are identical.

- **expert**: decides every tick; grants each resident's wish at once; claims every reward; tops up needs below 55; furnishes by cozy per button; greets the visitor every night; mends the door; leaves a gift every night. It never uses the light control (a hurried night is not modelled).
- **newcomer**: decides every 10 s and skips a third of those (seeded); claims rewards every 90 s; looks after needs and furnishes only after minute 2; first greets the visitor after minute 6; mends the door only after minute 15; never leaves a gift.

## What the numbers say

1. **No spending wall.** Nothing the bots want is ever out of reach for long: decor (136-144 in total) is finished by minute 5, the door (430) by minute 22-26, and a bot that waits on a step is waiting on bond or whispers, not on buttons.
2. **A quiet trough on day 1.** Income is spiky because wishes and rewards refresh at dawn, every 240 s. Day 1 pays 73 buttons in minute 1 (expert) and then only 8 buttons across minutes 2-3; the newcomer earns 6-12 per minute in minutes 2-4 until the dawn at minute 4 pays 84 in one minute. A new player has little to do for about three minutes after the opening burst except explore. Nothing is blocked (the exploring is the point, and the visitor arrives at minute 2), so this is a pacing note, not a bug.
3. **Cozy and decor are exhausted early.** Cozy reaches 100 at minute 4 and decor is complete at minute 5, so the later whisper gates (cozy 45-68) never bind. Whispers are limited by one per night, not by cozy.
4. **Late sink gap.** After the door opens (minute 22 expert, 26 newcomer) the only sink is a gift at 12 per night, while income stays near 25 per minute: the idle balance grows from 50 to 186 between minutes 22 and 30 (floating column in the script output). The expert's 13 milestones are all claimed by minute 22.
5. **Hurrying nights.** The light control can reach a night at once, and `discover` only needs a new day, so the one-per-night limit is cheap for a player who toggles the light. The bots do not do this; it is noted so nobody reads minute 22 as a hard schedule.

## Proposal (one, small, not implemented here)

The only item that looks like a real wall for a normal player is the day-1 trough (point 2): after the opening burst, wishes are spent until dawn at minute 4. One small option is to let each resident's wish rotate 90 s after it was granted, instead of only at dawn. It adds income, so it changes the curve above and the full-house streak rule; it should be decided by the owner and re-measured with this script before any change. Nothing else is proposed: points 3-4 are content-depth questions, not tuning.
