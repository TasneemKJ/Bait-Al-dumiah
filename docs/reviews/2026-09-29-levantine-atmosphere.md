## Reference boundaries
Palestinian embroidery uses geometric and plant motifs on linen, cotton and
wool; its designs can communicate specific identities. These small toy layouts
are original geometric compositions, not claims of authentic regional dress.
Reference: UNESCO, “The art of embroidery in Palestine, practices, skills,
knowledge and rituals”: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/01722

Damascus courtyard houses inform inward-facing windows, polychrome masonry and
a small water feature. This is a fictional Levantine-inspired home, not an
architectural reconstruction or a claim that all Levantine homes are the same.
Reference: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, “The Damascus Room”:
https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-damascus-room
