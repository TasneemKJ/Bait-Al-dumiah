## en-US

**Subtitle:** A cute and creepy dollhouse

**Promotional text:** A tiny home. Three little souls. A few beautiful secrets. Care for porcelain dolls, pour tea, and greet a shy visitor after dark.

**Keywords:** dollhouse,doll,cozy,tea,sewing,ghost,miniature,porcelain,levantine,arabic,cute,creepy,house

**Description:**
Bait Al-dumiah is a tiny Levantine home, cut away at the front: four lit rooms and three porcelain dolls waiting for someone to care for them.

• Care for Lina, Noor and Sami: tea, play, naps and reassurance. Each doll has a daily wish, a favourite room and a treasured keepsake.
• Pour tea by hand: grab the pot, tilt it over the cups and fill each one to its gold line.
• Sew with a real needle on the cloth, and sing to the moon with the chimes above the bed.
• Decorate with keepsakes and restore each room, stage by stage.
• After dark a shy visitor peeks out from under a sheet. Greet it to hear a whisper, and find what waits behind the closed door.

Cute and a little creepy, with no gore, no death and no jump scares. No ads, no purchases, no accounts, and the game never goes online. In English and Levantine Arabic.

## Review notes

No sign-in, no purchases and no network: the whole game is on the device. Tap Play; a short intro
runs (Skip at the bottom right). Follow the clue into a room. Touch an object once to select it,
then touch it again to act; drag what is in your hand onto the right object. House tools →
Little rituals → Play (Lina's mint tea) starts the tea table. The moon button at the top right turns evening on, and the
visitor appears. Everything can be reached without payment or login.
