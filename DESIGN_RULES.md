# Design rules

Target player: casual and mobile, enjoys gentle sims; wants clarity, warmth and small mysteries, not stress.

1. **Home, not punishment.** No death, gore, jump scares or offline need decay. Unease is atmosphere, never a fail state.
2. **Cute and creepy, Levantine.** Porcelain dolls, tea, sewing, jasmine, courtyard light; shy ghost; dread comes from implication.
3. **Rewards are earned and collected**: paid only on an explicit action; mistakes cost nothing; practice is free; no farming loops.
4. **Learn by playing.** One-time hints, visible cues that work without sound; no text walls.
5. **Low chrome.** The house is the focus; HUD shows what matters now and stays quiet otherwise.
6. **Touch first.** 44px targets, no hover or keyboard dependence, safe-area aware; keyboard alternatives exist.
7. **Bilingual and RTL.** Every string in English and Arabic; Levantine-warm Arabic; no mixed-language leaks.
8. **Accessible.** Reduced motion, sound optional, readable contrast, announced state changes.
9. **Safe saves.** Never lose progress; corruption falls back to a fresh house.
10. **Performance on phones.** Fit the triangle and draw-call budget; degrade detail before frame rate.
